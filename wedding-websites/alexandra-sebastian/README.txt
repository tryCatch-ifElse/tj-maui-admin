# Sample Wedding Invitation

Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the sample invitation.

## Customize
- Replace Alexandra and Sebastian with the couple's names.
- Update the date, venue, attire, entourage, and RSVP deadline.
- Replace the sample photos if desired.
- The RSVP form is demo-only. It does not save submissions. Connect it to your backend/database before using it for real guests.

## Publish
Upload `index.html` to your hosting provider or place it in your wedding website deployment workflow. The page uses Google Fonts and remote Unsplash photos, so an internet connection is needed for those assets.
